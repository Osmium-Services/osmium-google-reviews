**Google Reviews** shows reviews you choose on your site. You add them by hand, so only the ones you want to show appear.

## Add a review

1. Press **Add Review**.
2. Enter the **Author name** and choose the **Rating** from 1 to 5 stars.
3. Enter the **Review date**.
4. Paste the **Review text**, copying the wording exactly as it appears on Google.
5. **Display order** sets the position. A lower number shows first.
6. Tick **Show on site** and press **Save**.

## Show or hide a review

Use **Show** or **Hide** on the review's row. **Edit** changes it, and **Delete** moves it to **Deleted Reviews**, where **Purge All** removes them for good.

## Leave-a-review link

Press **Settings** and paste your **Review link**. In your Google Business Profile, open **Get more reviews**, then **Share review form**, to find it. Your site's design can then show a link that lets customers write a review. Leave it empty to hide the link.
